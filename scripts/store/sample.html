CHAPTER 02 · PROCESS

Clear documents make faster decisions

The teams we studied shipped 38% faster when every review happened on a single shared document, with comments, approvals and signatures in one place.

Most projects do not slow down because the work is hard. They slow down while people wait: for feedback, for a signature, for the latest version of a file that lives in someone else's inbox.

Across forty product teams, the pattern was the same. Reviews that started in a shared PDF closed in two days on average. Reviews that bounced between email threads took more than a week.

The best teams kept their documents short, marked them up directly, and resolved every comment before moving on. Decisions were written down where everyone could see them.

We also found that teams who signed off digitally, rather than printing and scanning, cut the final approval step from days to minutes — and never lost track of the latest version.
