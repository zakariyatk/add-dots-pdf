Privacy policy

ADD DOTS PDF · last updated 28 September 2026

Your documents stay on your device

ADD DOTS PDF opens, draws, edits and saves your PDFs on your own device, in the browser or in the app. Your documents are not uploaded to us unless you create a share link (below).

Your account

Signing in is optional. If you create an account, we keep:

  • your email address, and whether you confirmed it;
  • your password, stored only as a secure hash (we never see it);
  • when the account was created and when it last signed in, and the network address of each sign-in, to protect the account.

Accounts are run on Supabase (hosted in Mumbai, India), which sends the emails with your codes. Your account is used only to sign you in on the web, Windows, Android and iOS. We do not sell it, share it or use it for advertising.

Deleting your account

In the app, open Your account, then Delete account, and confirm with your password. The account and everything above are deleted at once, on every device. Documents on your devices are not touched.

When something does leave your device

  • Share links. Creating a link uploads that document, and the comments made on it, to our share server, so the people holding the link can open it. A shared document nobody has opened for 180 days is deleted.
  • Translate. The text you ask to translate is sent to the translation service you choose (Google Translate by default, or MyMemory).
  • Image search. The words you search for are sent to Openverse.

Kept only on this device

  • An error log, to help fix problems. File names are hidden and nothing is sent: you choose whether to copy it to us.
  • Unsaved work, so it can be offered back after a crash, until you restore or discard it.
  • Your sign-in, so you stay signed in, until you sign out.

There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Children

ADD DOTS PDF is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly keep an account for one.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it. For any question about your data, or to ask us to delete it, contact us through the support address on the app's store page.
